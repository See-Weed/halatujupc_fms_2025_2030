The Malaysian context

Why Palliative Medicine in Primary Care?

As Malaysia’s population ages and more people live with cancer, advanced organ disease, dementia and other serious illnesses, the need for accessible, continuous and holistic care is increasing.

Many patients and families navigate care between hospitals, Klinik Kesihatan and home. Primary care offers an opportunity to recognise palliative needs earlier, maintain continuity, support families and coordinate care with hospital specialist teams and community partners.

This is not about replacing specialist palliative care in hospitals. It is about strengthening the capacity of primary care to deliver appropriate palliative approaches and advanced services as part of the wider healthcare system.

Palliative care should not begin only when curative treatment ends. It can be integrated alongside ongoing disease management, according to patients’ needs and preferences.
Our professional contribution

The Role of Family Medicine

Family Medicine Specialists are positioned to provide longitudinal, person-centred care within Klinik Kesihatan and community settings, while collaborating with other disciplines and services.

01 · IDENTIFY

Earlier recognition

Identify serious illness and unmet palliative needs during routine clinical care, rather than waiting for the terminal phase.

02 · INTEGRATE

Holistic management

Address symptoms, function, emotional and social needs, values and goals of care alongside ongoing chronic disease management.

03 · CONNECT

Continuity of care

Coordinate clinic, hospital, home and community-based support for patients and their families.

04 · ADVANCE

Advanced clinical capability

Develop subspecialty-trained FMS to support more complex care, clinical leadership, training and service development.

05 · SUPPORT

Families and communities

Strengthen caregiver support, rehabilitation, community resources and home-based care where appropriate.

06 · COLLABORATE

Integrated networks

Work with hospital palliative medicine teams, multidisciplinary primary care providers and local partners.
