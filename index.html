Purpose & direction

General Objective

— To transform Malaysia’s healthcare system by shifting palliative care from a hospital-centric model into an accessible, community-based service integrated within primary care.

— This aims to ensure that all individuals facing life-limiting illnesses receive timely, equitable, holistic, and compassionate care—both at primary care clinics and in their homes—supported by competent healthcare teams, empowered families, and standardized quality frameworks.
ISSUE

Limited integration and development of comprehensive Palliative Medicine services within Malaysia's primary healthcare system.
