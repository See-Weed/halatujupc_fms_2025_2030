Advanced Specialty Palliative Medicine in Primary Care

Training structure

A structured training pathway combining clinical immersion, community-based practice, broader exposure, and implementation of palliative medicine services in Klinik Kesihatan.

Overview of Training

The training pathway comprises Clinical Immersion & Skill Development followed by Implementation & Assessment in Klinik Kesihatan.

Training structure reproduced from the supplied 2026 programme overview. The figures above each coloured block indicate months. AP and alternative posting arrangements are retained exactly as shown in the original diagram.

Training Pathway

The training overview describes two broad components: clinical immersion and skill development, followed by implementation and assessment in the primary care setting. The attachments below are presented according to the supplied 2026 overview.

01 · Clinical Immersion & Skill Development

Develop clinical capabilities through adult and paediatric palliative care, hospice and primary care attachments, and additional or optional postings.

02 · Implementation & Assessment

Apply acquired competencies in Klinik Kesihatan through service workflow development, independent clinical practice, leadership, education, and audit.

Training Attachments

Select attachments expose trainees to malignant and non-malignant illness, different age groups, multidisciplinary settings and the realities of Malaysian primary care.

019 months
Clinical immersion

Adult Palliative Care

Pain and symptom management; psychosocial and spiritual care; ethical decision-making; end-of-life care; exposure to neurological conditions, CKD, COPD and heart failure.

026 months
Clinical immersion

Paediatric Palliative Care

Paediatric symptom management; psychosocial and family-centred care; developmental considerations; ethical and legal aspects; congenital, neuromuscular and severe neurological conditions.

033–4 months
Clinical immersion

Hospice Attachment

Comprehensive symptom management; communication about goals of care; bereavement support; multidisciplinary collaboration; end-of-life guidance and non-malignant conditions.

043–4 months
Clinical immersion

Domiciliary & Primary Palliative Care in Klinik Kesihatan

Symptom and pain management in resource-limited settings; caregiver empowerment; emergency preparedness at home; culturally sensitive communication; community resources and primary care integration.

052 months
Additional posting

Geriatric Palliative Care

Symptom management in older adults; decision-making in frailty; family support; non-malignant palliative care.

061 month each
Optional posting

Optional Rotations

Oncology palliative care; wound care in palliative settings; rehabilitation; psychiatry; or another relevant posting.

074–6 months
Broader exposure

Overseas Training

Resource management; cultural sensitivity; innovative symptom management; psychosocial and spiritual support models.

086 months
Implementation & assessment

Klinik Kesihatan: Implementation & Assessment

Operationalising a palliative care workflow; independent symptom management; care planning; system navigation and leadership; community education; policy engagement; data collection and audit.
